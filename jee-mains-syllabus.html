Joint Entrance Examination (Main) — Physics • Chemistry • Mathematics

1. परीक्षा का परिचय

JEE Mains (Joint Entrance Examination Main) भारत में इंजीनियरिंग प्रवेश के लिए सबसे महत्वपूर्ण राष्ट्रीय स्तर की परीक्षा है। यह परीक्षा **NTA (National Testing Agency)** द्वारा आयोजित की जाती है। JEE Mains के माध्यम से छात्र **NITs, IIITs, GFTIs** और अन्य प्रमुख इंजीनियरिंग संस्थानों में प्रवेश पा सकते हैं।

JEE Mains में **Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech)** और **Paper 2 (B.Arch/B.Planning)** — दो पेपर होते हैं। यहाँ हम **Paper 1 (B.Tech)** पर केंद्रित रहेंगे जो सबसे अधिक छात्र देते हैं।

📌 महत्वपूर्ण जानकारी

  • परीक्षा का नाम: JEE Mains (Paper 1 — B.E./B.Tech)
  • आयोजक: NTA (National Testing Agency)
  • मोड: Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • कुल प्रश्न: 75 (25 Physics + 25 Chemistry + 25 Maths)
  • कुल अंक: 300
  • समय: 3 घंटे
  • मुख्य वेबसाइट: jeemain.nta.nic.in

3. पात्रता एवं आयु सीमा

3.1 शैक्षिक योग्यता

  • 10+2 (Intermediate) में Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics अनिवार्य
  • Minimum 75% marks (General/OBC) या top 20 percentile
  • SC/ST के लिए 65% marks

3.2 आयु सीमा

  • कोई आयु सीमा नहीं है
  • लगातार 3 वर्ष तक परीक्षा दे सकते हैं
  • 12वीं के बाद 2 साल तक

4. Physics विस्तृत सिलेबस

4.1 Mechanics

  • Units & Measurements — SI Units, Dimensions, Error Analysis
  • Kinematics — Motion in 1D & 2D, Projectile Motion, Relative Velocity
  • Laws of Motion — Newton's Laws, Friction, Circular Motion
  • Work, Energy & Power — Work-Energy Theorem, Conservative Forces
  • Rotational Motion — Moment of Inertia, Torque, Angular Momentum
  • Gravitation — Kepler's Laws, Gravitational Potential Energy

4.2 Thermodynamics & Kinetic Theory

  • Thermal Properties — Thermal Expansion, Calorimetry, Heat Transfer
  • Thermodynamics — Laws of Thermodynamics, Carnot Engine, Entropy
  • Kinetic Theory — Ideal Gas Equation, Mean Free Path, Degrees of Freedom

4.3 Oscillations & Waves

  • Simple Harmonic Motion — SHM Equations, Energy in SHM
  • Waves — Wave Equation, Superposition, Standing Waves
  • Sound — Doppler Effect, Beats, Resonance

4.4 Electrostatics & Current Electricity

  • Electric Charges & Fields — Coulomb's Law, Gauss's Law
  • Electric Potential — Potential Energy, Equipotential Surfaces
  • Capacitors — Parallel & Series, Dielectrics, Energy Stored
  • Current Electricity — Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, Wheatstone Bridge

4.5 Magnetism & EMI

  • Magnetic Effects of Current — Biot-Savart Law, Ampere's Law
  • Magnetism & Matter — Dia, Para, Ferromagnetic Materials
  • EMI — Faraday's Laws, Lenz's Law, Self & Mutual Induction
  • AC Circuits — LCR Circuits, Resonance, Power Factor

4.6 Optics

  • Ray Optics — Reflection, Refraction, Lenses, Prism, Optical Instruments
  • Wave Optics — Interference, Diffraction, Polarization, Young's Double Slit

4.7 Modern Physics

  • Dual Nature — Photoelectric Effect, de Broglie Waves
  • Atoms — Bohr Model, Hydrogen Spectrum
  • Nuclei — Radioactivity, Nuclear Fission & Fusion
  • Semiconductors — Diodes, Transistors, Logic Gates

5. Chemistry विस्तृत सिलेबस

5.1 Physical Chemistry

  • Some Basic Concepts — Mole Concept, Stoichiometry
  • Atomic Structure — Quantum Numbers, Orbitals, Electronic Configuration
  • Chemical Bonding — Ionic, Covalent, VSEPR, Hybridization, MOT
  • States of Matter — Gas Laws, Liquid State, Solid State
  • Thermodynamics — Enthalpy, Entropy, Gibbs Free Energy
  • Equilibrium — Chemical & Ionic Equilibrium, pH, Buffer
  • Redox Reactions — Oxidation Number, Balancing
  • Solutions — Concentration, Colligative Properties
  • Electrochemistry — Electrochemical Cells, Nernst Equation
  • Chemical Kinetics — Rate Laws, Order of Reaction, Arrhenius Equation
  • Surface Chemistry — Adsorption, Catalysis, Colloids

5.2 Inorganic Chemistry

  • Periodic Table — Periodic Properties, Trends
  • p-Block Elements — Group 13-18
  • d & f Block Elements — Transition Elements, Lanthanides, Actinides
  • Coordination Compounds — Werner's Theory, Nomenclature, CFT
  • Metallurgy — Extraction of Metals
  • Qualitative Analysis — Cations & Anions

5.3 Organic Chemistry

  • Basics — IUPAC Nomenclature, Isomerism, Resonance
  • Hydrocarbons — Alkanes, Alkenes, Alkynes, Aromatic
  • Haloalkanes & Haloarenes — SN1, SN2 Reactions
  • Alcohols, Phenols & Ethers — Preparation, Properties, Reactions
  • Aldehydes, Ketones & Carboxylic Acids
  • Amines — Preparation, Basic Character, Diazonium Salts
  • Biomolecules — Carbohydrates, Proteins, Vitamins, Nucleic Acids
  • Polymers — Classification, Important Polymers
  • Chemistry in Everyday Life — Drugs, Detergents, Food Additives

6. Mathematics विस्तृत सिलेबस

6.1 Algebra

  • Complex Numbers — Argand Plane, Modulus, Conjugate
  • Quadratic Equations — Roots, Discriminant, Nature of Roots
  • Sequences & Series — AP, GP, HP, Special Series
  • Permutations & Combinations
  • Binomial Theorem — General Term, Middle Term
  • Matrices & Determinants — Types, Operations, Properties
  • Mathematical Induction

6.2 Trigonometry

  • Trigonometric Ratios & Identities
  • Trigonometric Equations
  • Inverse Trigonometric Functions
  • Heights & Distances

6.3 Calculus

  • Limits, Continuity & Differentiability
  • Differentiation — Chain Rule, Implicit, Logarithmic
  • Applications of Derivatives — Maxima, Minima, Tangents, Normals
  • Integration — Substitution, By Parts, Partial Fractions
  • Definite Integrals — Properties, Area Under Curve
  • Differential Equations — Order, Degree, Solution

6.4 Coordinate Geometry

  • Straight Lines — Slope, Various Forms, Distance
  • Circles — Equation, Tangent, Normal, Chord
  • Conic Sections — Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola
  • 3D Geometry — Direction Cosines, Plane, Straight Line

6.5 Vectors & Statistics

  • Vector Algebra — Types, Operations, Dot & Cross Product
  • Statistics — Mean, Median, Mode, Variance, Standard Deviation
  • Probability — Conditional, Bayes Theorem, Random Variables
