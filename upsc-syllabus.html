PART A — PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION

1. CURRENT EVENTS OF NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL IMPORTANCE

  • Government policies and programmes

  • Gandhian movements
  • Quit India Movement
  • Constitutional developments
  • Indian independence and partition

  • Climatology
  • Atmosphere and weather systems

  • Oceanography
  • Rivers, lakes and water resources
  • Soils and natural vegetation
  • Indian agriculture and cropping patterns
  • Population and human geography
  • Urbanisation and migration
  • Industries and location factors

  • Constitutional amendments
  • Union and State governments

  • Emergency provisions
  • Election Commission
  • Comptroller and Auditor General
  • Union Public Service Commission

  • Panchayati Raj
  • Urban local government
  • Public policy
  • Governance and transparency
  • Rights issues
5. ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT
  • Indian economy — basic concepts

  • National income

  • Banking system

  • National parks and wildlife sanctuaries

UPSC → Preliminary → GS-I / CSAT
UPSC → Mains → Essay → GS-I → GS-II → GS-III → GS-IV → Qualifying Papers → Optional
