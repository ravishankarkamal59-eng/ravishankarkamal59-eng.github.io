कोर्स का परिचय

BA English में British Literature, American Literature, Indian Writing in English और Grammar का अध्ययन होता है।

📖 सिलेबस (Subject-wise)

  • British Literature (Poetry, Drama, Novel)
  • American Literature
  • Indian Writing in English
  • Literary Criticism & Theory
  • English Grammar & Composition
  • Communication Skills

💼 करियर ऑप्शन

  • English Teacher (TGT, PGT)
  • UGC NET / Professor
  • Content Writer
  • Translator
  • Civil Services (optional subject)
  • Media & Journalism

  • प्रमुख Universities: DU, BHU, JNU, AMU, Allahabad University, Patna University
