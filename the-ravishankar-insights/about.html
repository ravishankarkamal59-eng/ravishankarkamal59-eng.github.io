About The Ravishankar Insights

The Ravishankar Insights is an educational platform created to help students access useful study materials, syllabus information, examination resources, articles and learning content in one place.

Our aim is to make quality educational resources easier to find and accessible to students preparing for university examinations and competitive examinations.

The platform covers educational resources related to areas such as BA, MA, UPSC, UPPCS, SSC, Railway, Banking, NET-JRF and other examinations.
